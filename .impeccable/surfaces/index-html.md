Scope: index.html, the whole single-page CV site at dave.prow.se.

Visitor mode: Persuade. The visitor decides whether to start a conversation.

Audience and job: executive search and in-house recruiters, hiring executives and
boards, and senior security peers. All arrive from a link Dave sent or that was
passed on; none arrive from search. The recruiter skims for level, scope and the
build record before deciding to read.

Action: email hiring@prow.se, or download the PDF to circulate internally. The
PDF is the formal artefact; the page must never contradict it.

Proof and content: the CV text is the entire evidentiary record. No photo, no
imagery, no talks, writing, repos or press exist. Nothing may be fabricated. The
700 million players figure is a real public EA number and has no siblings.

Constraints: single self-contained HTML file, no build step; noindex/nofollow plus
the robots.txt disallow stay; the email is assembled at runtime in JS; print is a
first-class path because recruiters print this. WCAG 2.2 AA is binding.

Chosen direction: "The Considered Record". Near-monochrome restraint on white,
one contrast-derived orange accent, modular type scale, hairline rules, mono for
data only. Arrived at after the user rejected an industrial machine-rating-plate
direction as reading like early-2000s Geocities; structure and density then
followed a user-supplied reference, luciano-pereira.pages.dev.

Memorable moment: the masthead. Name at display scale, role line, one-sentence
lede, a mono middot contact row, then a single orange action, all above a
five-field facts strip that answers level, location, scope, focus and tenure in
one glance.

Decisions of record:
- Imitation material is banned outright (no bevels, embossing, faux-3D, gradient
  metal). This is what the rejected direction got wrong.
- Named standards stay stamped against the Dyson role that applied them, never
  hoisted to career scope.
- The capabilities grid regroups facts already present in the role text and
  introduces no new claims.

Unresolved: no photograph or signature mark has been supplied; if one ever is, the
masthead is where it would go. The user's own phrase "mission-critical" is flagged
by the detector as a buzzword and is left as-is pending their call.
